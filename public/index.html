The Fitness App Fit For The Modern Parent

Welcome to FamBod! Our goal is to help busy and tired moms and dads stay committed to their fitness and health. By adding fun and short workouts into the quality time you spend with your little ones, you can keep your Mom and Dad Bods fit and healthy.

How Does It Work?

A Fitness Plan, Just For You

Tell us a little about you and your child, and we'll build a personal plan that grows with you.

Exercise Without Skipping a Beat

Short workouts you do together with your child, at playtime or bedtime, without missing a moment.

Stay on Track

Quick exercises you can squeeze in anytime, with friendly reminders to keep you going.

Progress, Step by Step

Every week you get a little stronger, and FamBod tracks your progress and grows your plan with you.
