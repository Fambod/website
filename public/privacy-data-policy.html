FamBod Privacy Policy

Version 2.0 — Effective date: November 8, 2026

The short version

This summary is for convenience; the full policy below is the complete notice.

  • We collect what we need to run FamBod: your account details (or sign-in via Google or Apple), your role (“Mom” or “Dad”), your in-app activity and preferences so we can personalize your experience, and standard technical and security data.
  • The only child-related item in the product is the safety confirmation you give as a parent (“…I confirm my child is at least 6 months old and can hold their head up”) — recorded as your acknowledgment of the safety notice, not as data about your child.
  • We use analytics, including session replay, to understand how FamBod is used and to improve it. It starts once you accept our Terms and this policy, and you can turn it off at any time with the Share usage data toggle in Settings.
  • We do not sell personal data. Our vendors process data only on our instructions, under contract.
  • You can delete your account, and your data with it, in the app or by email (Section 9 explains what is removed and what we must keep).
  • The data controller is FAMBOD LTD, an Israeli company. Contact: support@fambod.com.

2. What we collect

We tell you in the app which information is required: account information is necessary to create an account and use FamBod — without it, we cannot provide the Service. Everything beyond your account information is optional.

2.1 Account information

Your email address, name, and password (never stored in plain text) — or, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the information you authorize that provider to share with us, typically your name, email address, and basic profile details. (Apple may provide a private relay email address instead of your real one.) Those providers act under their own privacy policies for the sign-in itself, and you can control what they share through their settings.

2.2 Profile information

Your self-assigned role (“Mom” or “Dad”), which we use to tailor workouts and content to you, and, if you choose to add them, your country and phone number.

2.3 Activity and preferences

Your in-app activity (such as which exercises and workout plans you start, complete, or stop, and your progress) and your settings (such as language, notification preferences, and other customization choices). We use this to deliver the core product: personalized exercise plans, relevant content and notifications, and an app that remembers how you like it. This is first-party data processed to provide the Service, separate from the usage analytics in Section 2.4.

2.4 Usage analytics and session replay

Once you accept our Terms and this policy, we collect usage analytics through PostHog. Nothing is sent before you accept. This data is pseudonymous: PostHog never receives your name, email address, or other contact details, only a random FamBod user ID, so the data in PostHog cannot identify you on its own. Analytics covers the screens you view, the buttons you tap, and workout activity such as exercises started, completed, or stopped, together with that user ID, your role (“Mom” or “Dad”), and your country. We use this to understand how FamBod is used and to improve it.

Session replay. Analytics includes session replay: a visual reconstruction of how you move through the app’s screens, which we use to find bugs and improve the design. Recordings exclude personal information: anything you type, your name, your email address, and other personal details are hidden and never captured.

Turning it off. Switch off the Share usage data toggle in Settings. Usage analytics and session replay stop on that device straight away.

2.5 Notifications and marketing

If you allow notifications, we send workout reminders through our notification provider (Section 5), using a device push token. You can turn them off in Settings or in your device’s settings.

We send tips, updates, and offers by email only if you have agreed to receive them: we will ask first, and every marketing email has an unsubscribe link. When we engage a marketing platform, it will act as our processor and we will name it in Section 5. Service communications (such as security notices or changes to these documents) are not marketing.

2.6 Technical and diagnostic data

Standard server logs and technical information: IP address, timestamps, device and operating system information, and app version. The app also sends crash reports, error reports, app logs, and performance measurements (such as how long a screen takes to load), linked to your account so we can trace a problem to the account it affected. We use this data to operate, secure, and fix the Service and to prevent fraud and abuse.

2.7 Consent records

When you accept our Terms, this policy, or the safety notice, or change the Share usage data toggle or your marketing choice, we record your user ID, what you accepted or changed, the document version, and when. We keep these records to show what you agreed to and to honor your choices.

2.8 Scope of collection

Sections 2.1–2.7 are the complete picture of what FamBod collects. Children are not users of FamBod and are not data subjects of this Service; the only child-related element in the product is a safety acknowledgment you give as a parent (“…I confirm my child is at least 6 months old and can hold their head up”), which we record as your acceptance of the safety notice, not as information about your child.

4. Your choices

When you create an account, you tick one box to agree to our Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy. The same box appears if you sign up with Google or Apple.

After that, you control usage analytics and session replay (the Share usage data toggle in Settings, Section 2.4), notifications (in Settings or your device’s settings), and marketing emails (Section 2.5). Changes take effect going forward and don’t affect processing before the change. The Terms of Service and this policy are linked from Settings.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights including:

  • Access to the personal data we hold about you, and a copy of it (including portability of data you provided, where GDPR applies);
  • Correction of inaccurate data;
  • Deletion of your data, in the circumstances applicable law provides;
  • Restriction of or objection to certain processing, as applicable law provides;
  • Withdrawal of consent at any time for consent-based processing (Section 4);
  • Complaint to a supervisory authority — your local EU/EEA data protection authority, the UK Information Commissioner’s Office, or the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority.

To exercise any right, use the in-app controls where available (Settings; account deletion) or email support@fambod.com. We may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law. Exercising your rights is free of charge except where the law allows otherwise.

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app, or by emailing support@fambod.com from the address on your account. Deletion removes your account, profile, and activity, and deletes your analytics data with our providers, within the windows in Section 7. Consent records and data we must keep by law are retained as described in Section 7. Deletion cannot be undone.

10. Security

We protect your data with industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and contractual security obligations on our processors. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security — but if we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will act in accordance with applicable breach notification laws.

11. Children

FamBod is for adults aged 18 and over. Children may not create accounts and are not users of the Service, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children (see Section 2.8 on the safety acknowledgment). If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact support@fambod.com and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. The version number and effective date at the top show the current version, and we keep previous versions.
