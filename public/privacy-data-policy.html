FamBod Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 — Effective date: September 30, 2026

The short version

This summary is for convenience; the full policy below is the complete notice.

  • We collect what we need to run FamBod: your account details (or sign-in via Google, Apple, or Facebook), a minimal profile (your date of birth and your role — “Mom” or “Dad”), your in-app activity and preferences so we can personalize your experience, and standard technical and security data.
  • The only child-related item in the product is the safety confirmation you give as a parent (“…I confirm my child is at least 6 months old and can hold their head up”) — recorded as your acknowledgment of the safety notice, not as data about your child.
  • Usage analytics and marketing happen only if you opt in, via two separate toggles that are off by default and never affect your access to the app. You can switch either off at any time in Settings.
  • We do not sell personal data. Our vendors process data only on our instructions, under contract.
  • You can delete your account, and your data with it, from inside the app (Section 9 explains what is removed and what we must keep).
  • The data controller is FAMBOD LTD, an Israeli company. Contact: support@fambod.com.

2. What we collect

We tell you in the app which information is required: account information is necessary to create an account and use FamBod — without it, we cannot provide the Service. The opt-in items in Sections 2.4 and 2.5 are entirely voluntary, and declining them has no consequence for your use of the app.

2.1 Account information

Your email address, name, and password (never stored in plain text) — or, if you sign in with Google, Apple, or Facebook, the information you authorize that provider to share with us, typically your name, email address, and basic profile details. (Apple may provide a private relay email address instead of your real one.) Those providers act under their own privacy policies for the sign-in itself, and you can control what they share through their settings.

2.2 Profile information

Your date of birth and your self-assigned role (“Mom” or “Dad”). We use these to tailor workouts and content to you, and we use your date of birth to verify that you meet the minimum age requirement (18+).

2.3 Activity and preferences

Your in-app activity (such as which exercises and workout plans you start, complete, or stop, and your progress) and your settings (such as language, notification preferences, and other customization choices). We use this to deliver the core product: personalized exercise plans, relevant content and notifications, and an app that remembers how you like it. This is first-party data processed to provide the Service — it is separate from the optional analytics below and does not depend on any opt-in.

2.4 Usage analytics — only if you opt in

If you switch on the analytics toggle (“Share usage data to help improve FamBod”), we collect product analytics through PostHog: a pseudonymous user identifier and behavioral events such as screen views, button taps, and exercise started/completed/stopped events. We use this solely to understand how FamBod is used and to improve it.

Session replay. Analytics may also include session replay: a reconstruction of how the app’s post-signup workout screens were used, with all text inputs and personal details masked so they are never captured. Onboarding screens are never recorded. Session replay is covered by the same analytics consent — if your analytics toggle is on, it may include replay; if it is off, no analytics of any kind, including replay, are collected.

You can switch analytics off at any time in Settings, which stops collection going forward (see Section 4 for how consent works).

2.5 Marketing — only if you opt in

If you switch on the marketing toggle (“Send me FamBod tips and updates by email and notifications”), we send you product tips, updates, and promotions by email and push notification. You can withdraw at any time — in Settings, via the unsubscribe link in every marketing email, or by disabling notifications in your device settings (see Section 4). When we engage a marketing platform, it will act as our processor and we will name it in Section 5. Service communications (such as security notices or material changes to these documents) are not marketing and are sent regardless of this toggle.

2.6 Technical and security data

Standard server logs and technical information: IP address, timestamps, device and operating system information, and app version. We use these to operate, secure, and debug the Service and to prevent fraud and abuse. If you enable push notifications, a device push token is processed through our notification provider (Section 5) to deliver them; you control notifications in the app’s settings and your device’s OS settings.

2.7 Consent records

Every consent action you take — granting, declining, or withdrawing — is logged append-only with: your user ID, the action, a UTC timestamp, the document type, version, and exact text or content hash you saw, your locale, app version, platform, IP address, and user agent. We keep these records to demonstrate that consent was validly given and to honor your choices accurately. Documents are versioned and archived, and material changes trigger re-acceptance in the app.

2.8 Scope of collection

Sections 2.1–2.7 are the complete picture of what FamBod collects. Children are not users of FamBod and are not data subjects of this Service; the only child-related element in the product is a safety acknowledgment you give as a parent (“…I confirm my child is at least 6 months old and can hold their head up”), which we record as your acceptance of the safety notice, not as information about your child.

4. Consent: how it works and how to withdraw it

At signup you see one required checkbox (agreeing to the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy — required to create an account) and two separate, optional toggles, off by default: analytics and marketing. The optional toggles never block signup or any functionality.

You can change either toggle at any time in Settings. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it, takes effect going forward, and doesn’t affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. The required items are viewable in Settings with links to the current documents.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights including:

  • Access to the personal data we hold about you, and a copy of it (including portability of data you provided, where GDPR applies);
  • Correction of inaccurate data;
  • Deletion of your data, in the circumstances applicable law provides;
  • Restriction of or objection to certain processing, as applicable law provides;
  • Withdrawal of consent at any time for consent-based processing (Section 4);
  • Complaint to a supervisory authority — your local EU/EEA data protection authority, the UK Information Commissioner’s Office, or the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority.

To exercise any right, use the in-app controls where available (Settings; account deletion) or email support@fambod.com. We may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law. Exercising your rights is free of charge except where the law allows otherwise.

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account directly in the app. Deletion permanently removes your account, profile, and activity data and disassociates or deletes your analytics data with our processors, within the windows in Section 7. Consent records and data we must keep for legal compliance are retained as described above. Deletion cannot be undone.

10. Security

We protect your data with industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and contractual security obligations on our processors. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security — but if we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will act in accordance with applicable breach notification laws.

11. Children

FamBod is for adults aged 18 and over. Children may not create accounts and are not users of the Service, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children (see Section 2.8 on the safety acknowledgment). If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact support@fambod.com and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy is versioned, and previous versions are archived. If we make a material change, we will notify you and ask you to review and accept the updated policy in the app before it applies to you; we record that acceptance as described in Section 2.7. Non-material changes take effect when posted, with the new version number and effective date shown below.
